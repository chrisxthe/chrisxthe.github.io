Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-11-21

gogcli-personal is a personal command-line tool operated solely by its owner (@chrisxthe) for accessing their own Google Calendar data from their own local workstation.

  • No Third-Party Data Collection: This tool is not offered as a public service and does not collect, store, sell, or share data from any third-party users on remote servers.

  • Limited Use Compliance: Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
